Hypothesis

A reasoning-mode LLM (Thinking ON) will become more overconfident on factual questions as those events approach its training cutoff — more so than the same model without reasoning (Thinking OFF).

Dataset

50 hand-curated open-ended factual questions across 5 temporal buckets (B1–B5), 10 per bucket.

Platform & Cost

Kaggle free tier · GPU T4 ×2 (~31 GB VRAM) · $0 cost · Full run takes ~2–3 hours

Limitations
  • N=50 — too small for statistical significance
  • Single model family — no cross-model claims
  • Verbalized confidence is noisy (self-reported numbers ≠ real probabilities)
  • Thinking OFF is methodologically awkward
  • Training cutoff is approximate
